Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can you complete my order?

Most orders are completed within 24-48 hours. Rush orders can often be accommodated with same-day service for an additional fee.

Do you offer design services?

Yes! Our creative team can help design your project from scratch or work with your existing ideas to create something amazing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, M-Pesa, bank transfers, and major credit cards. Payment terms can be discussed for larger orders.

Can you handle large quantity orders?

Absolutely! We have experience handling orders from single items to thousands of units. Contact us for volume pricing.

Do you provide delivery services?

Yes, we offer delivery within Nairobi and surrounding areas. Delivery fees apply based on location and order size.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

We guarantee 100% satisfaction. If you're not happy with the results, we'll work with you to make it right or provide a refund.
